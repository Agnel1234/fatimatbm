## Design Improvements

### Color Palette
- **Before:** Black text, white background (no theme)
- **After:** Professional Navy/Teal/Gold scheme matching your app

### Typography
- **Before:** Single font size throughout
- **After:** Clear hierarchy (18pt title → 8pt body)

### Spacing & Layout
- **Before:** Compact, minimal whitespace
- **After:** Professional margins, generous spacing

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Before:** All data equal importance
- **After:** Important metrics highlighted, organized sections

### Professional Appearance
- **Before:** Looks like raw database export
- **After:** Looks like a professional business report

### Data Insights
- **Before:** Just lists data
- **After:** Provides insights (collection rate, top dues, stats)
